## Overview

Version 1.0 is the Knowledge Base production-ready MVP for employee-managed customer campaigns. It
combines CRM records, products, consent and contact safeguards, segmentation, campaign approval and
launch, communications, reminders and follow-up work, analytics, AI-assisted recommendations, audit
history, role-based administration, and production operations in one Spring Boot and React system.

AI remains assistive: it cannot approve campaigns, override consent, bypass do-not-contact or
`EligibilityService`, or replace required human approval for campaign copy.

### Intended Audience And Support Boundary

Version 1.0 is an internal employee platform for the Admin, Campaign Manager, BI Analyst, Product
Manager, Compliance Officer, Customer Service Agent, Sales Agent, Marketing Analyst, Executive
Viewer, and System Auditor roles defined by the KB. It is not a public customer portal. Production
operation requires trained owners for deployment, security/compliance, providers, database/storage,
monitoring, backup/recovery, and incidents.

The support baseline is the documented Docker Compose deployment. Hosted monitoring, paging,
registry, secret manager, certificate automation, and off-host backup storage are deployment-owned
external services and are not bundled into the application repository.

## Product Capabilities

### Customers, Products, And Consent

- Searchable customer/prospect profiles with status, contact details, beneficiaries, product
  ownership, payment history, communication history, reminders, follow-up tasks, and soft deletion.
- Product catalog and ownership/change workflows with status, pricing, duration, and expiration data.
- Consent recording and withdrawal, evidence storage, guardian/marketing rules, opt-out and
  do-not-contact handling, contact-frequency limits, and auditable history.
- Recipient eligibility centralizes consent, do-not-contact, uninterested, retry, and monthly contact
  safeguards before communication.

### Segments And Campaigns

- Campaign Managers can view, create, edit, preview, and save segments. Segment UUIDs are generated
  automatically in valid UUID format and shown in the details panel.
- Campaign creation and editing include product selection, segment targeting, objective, message,
  schedule, status, recipient preview, exclusion reasons, and confirmation for sensitive actions.
- Campaigns follow draft, submission, human compliance review, approval/rejection, and controlled
  launch states. Approval and launch produce immutable audit records.
- Duplicate-contact warnings and eligibility checks cannot be bypassed by AI or UI flows.

### Communications, Reminders, And Follow-Up

- Communication history and contact outcomes support campaign/customer traceability and retry rules.
- Payment and product-expiration reminder processing includes Green/Yellow/Red levels, scheduled and
  controlled manual triggers, scheduler run/attempt logs, and payment-complete exclusions.
- Follow-up tasks can be created, assigned, filtered, updated, and completed.
- Email and SMS provider policy fails closed: real delivery remains disabled until an approved real
  provider and credentials are configured.

### Analytics, AI, And Audit

- Role-aware dashboards and reports expose campaign, engagement, conversion, ROI, product, segment,
  and compliance views without granting unauthorized raw-data access.
- AI customer search supports fuzzy/weighted matching and score explanation.
- Product recommendations, segment suggestions, default-risk scoring, duplicate-contact risk, and
  campaign-copy suggestions store explanations and confidence when available.
- Campaign-copy suggestions require explicit human approval.
- Immutable audit logs cover sensitive user, role, consent, campaign, provider/workflow, and export
  actions. Admin, Compliance Officer, and System Auditor reads are authorized and normal users cannot
  edit the audit trail.

### User Experience And Accessibility

- Responsive application shell with role-based sidebar, top bar/user menu, page headings, status
  badges, loading/empty/error states, success notifications, validation messages, and confirmation
  dialogs.
- Keyboard navigation, form labels, table labels, tablet/desktop responsiveness, minimum mobile
  usability, and contrast-oriented status presentation.

## Security And Production Operations

- BCrypt password hashing, JWT authentication, account status/lock controls, method/API authorization,
  restricted production CORS, HTTPS/HSTS, safe error responses, secret validation, and no production
  stack traces.
- Production Docker Compose includes PostgreSQL 16, backend, frontend, Nginx reverse proxy, persistent
  database and consent-evidence volumes, health checks, read-only application filesystems where
  applicable, bounded container logging, and internal database networking.
- One-time production administrator bootstrap is explicit, password-hashed, audited, idempotent, and
  disabled after use; migration-seeded `.test` accounts are quarantined by bootstrap processing.
- Scheduled PostgreSQL custom-format backups use atomic publication, archive validation, SHA-256
  manifests, retention, health markers, and a separate backup volume requiring encrypted off-host copy.
- Documented restore rehearsal, production restore, smoke gate, rollback, incident response, and
  operational monitoring processes support NFR-004, NFR-013, and NFR-014.

## Deployment And Upgrade Notes

This is the first `v1.0` production milestone, not an in-place upgrade promise from an undocumented
pre-release environment. Preserve production data before deployment, use only version-controlled
Flyway migrations, and validate the exact release in a production-like environment. Operators must
follow the [production deployment guide](../deployment/production-deployment-guide.md), not infer a
deployment from these notes.

### Runtime Baseline

- Java 21 and Maven for the Spring Boot backend.
- Node/npm and Vite for the React frontend, using the versions locked by the repository.
- PostgreSQL 16 with Flyway-managed schema migrations.
- Docker Engine and Docker Compose for the production deployment model.

### Database And Persistent Data

1. Before deployment, create and verify a new backup with item 735 and retain its encrypted off-host
   copy. Confirm item 736 restore rehearsal evidence for the selected recovery point.
2. Record the matching `bwc_consent_evidence` recovery point.
3. Review all Flyway migrations through `V25`; never edit an applied migration, run `flyway clean`,
   or improvise reverse DDL.
4. Start PostgreSQL and backend first. Require successful Flyway validation and readiness before
   frontend/proxy startup.
5. Follow the item 739 rollback plan if schema compatibility or critical smoke checks fail.

### Required Production Configuration

Provision values through the deployment secret manager and uncommitted environment file. Key groups
include database credentials, strong `JWT_SECRET`, explicit HTTPS CORS origins, TLS certificate/key
paths, contact/retry limits, scheduler cron/zone, provider modes/credentials, consent storage,
logging, backups, and optional one-time admin bootstrap.

Do not copy example placeholders into production. Do not include rendered Compose configuration,
secret values, provider credentials, or database URLs with credentials in release evidence.

## Known Limitations And Operational Decisions

- The repository does not bundle a hosted uptime monitor, centralized log/error platform, metrics
  database, paging service, host-capacity monitor, certificate monitor, or cloud point-in-time recovery.
  Operators must integrate approved external services; Docker/CLI checks are a project-scale fallback.
- PostgreSQL logical backups are the required baseline. Local backup volume health does not prove an
  encrypted off-host copy or successful restore.
- Real email/SMS delivery is disabled until provider activation is approved and safely configured.
- Production smoke testing requires approved role accounts and synthetic-only customer/campaign data.
- This draft does not claim a deployed environment, green final-main CI, smoke completion, image
  publication, or `v1.0` tag.
- No backward database migration is provided. Recovery uses a compatible immutable application
  image or a verified paired database/consent-evidence restore under human approval.
- AI outputs are decision support, may be incomplete, and require their documented human review;
  they do not expand authorization or replace deterministic eligibility controls.
